Universal Studios Hollywood

Theme park & movie studio — rides, shows, studio tour

📍 Google Maps
💡 Creator tip from @thecoasterfanman

Bring a small bag. The lockers for the Mummy and Harry Potter ride are TINY. Bring comfy shoes, a refillable water bottle, a portable battery, a portable fan (if you get hot easily — very little shade in the park), and some ear plugs (it gets very loud in some areas, and if you're sensitive to noise like me, it's a game changer).
